# Kesko Corporation — Precedent Transaction Analysis

A comprehensive precedent transaction comparables model for Kesko Corporation (KESKOB.HE) analyzing 16 European retail, grocery, and building materials M&A transactions from 2021-2025, built following CFI Financial Modeling Best Practices and Financial Modeling Guidelines.

**Precedent EV/EBITDA range: 6.0x–8.4x (median 7.4x). Adjusted implied price: EUR 17.94/share.**

## M&A Market Conditions (2020-2025)

| Year | Median EV/EBITDA | Environment | Key Theme |
|------|-----------------|-------------|-----------|
| 2020 | N/A | COVID disruption | Pipeline frozen; grocery defensive; PE dry powder accumulated |
| 2021 | 9.0x | Recovery boom | CD&R/Morrison GBP 9.8B; Couche-Tard/Carrefour blocked; low rates |
| 2022 | 8.3x | Rate shock | ECB/BoE hikes; CVC/Zabka showed convenience premium |
| 2023 | 6.9x | Buyer caution | Higher financing costs; strategic bolt-ons dominated |
| 2024 | 6.8x | Stabilization | Rates peaked; Kesko/Davidsen; strategic median 14.7x (record) |
| 2025 | 7.0x | Recovery | European M&A value +12% yoy; Kroger-Albertsons block tightens grocery antitrust |

The clear trend: multiples compressed from 9.0x (2021) to 6.8x (2024) as interest rates rose, then began recovering in 2025 as rates eased. Financial sponsors paid consistently higher multiples than strategics throughout the cycle.

## Premium / Discount Adjustments for Kesko

| Factor | Assessment | Direction |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| EBITDA Margin (9.1% vs. ~5-8% targets) | Superior profitability | **PREMIUM** |
| Revenue Scale (EUR 12.5B) | Scarcity value | **PREMIUM** |
| Market Position (#1-2 Finland) | Dominant position | **PREMIUM** |
| Balance Sheet (0.8x ND/EBITDA) | Under-levered | **PREMIUM** |
| Diversification (3 divisions) | Reduced risk | **PREMIUM** |
| B&T Cyclicality (36% of revenue) | Trough earnings | **DISCOUNT** |
| Geographic Concentration (60% Finland) | Single-country risk | **DISCOUNT** |
| IFRS 16 Lease Load (EUR 2.15B) | Reduces equity bridge | **DISCOUNT** |
| Strategic Buyer Premium (20-40%) | Only in M&A scenario | ADJUST |
| Liquidity (public company) | No discount needed | NEUTRAL |

**Net: 5 premium, 3 discount, 1 adjustment, 1 neutral. 15% premium to median warranted.**
